Vehicle Condition & Purchase Policy

How vehicle evaluation works

Vehicles are offered through dealer-only auctions with restricted access.

Because these auctions are not open to the general public, customers cannot visit, inspect, or test-drive vehicles before purchase.

Remote purchase decisions are made using the same type of information available to dealers, including auction photos, listing details, condition information, and CARFAX when the purchase reaches a more advanced stage.

Additional local verification

When a vehicle is located at an auction in Fort Lauderdale or Palm Beach, My Car Scout may also provide an optional in-person visual check.

This can include additional photos, video, and visual observations of the vehicle.

This service provides an additional layer of information before a purchase decision, but it does not replace a professional mechanical inspection.

Vehicle condition

Vehicles are purchased under the conditions disclosed by the auction.

My Car Scout does not repair or recondition vehicles before delivery.

This purchasing model creates the opportunity to buy on the wholesale side of the market, before retail markup is added, while accepting the conditions associated with that market.

Transparency before you decide

Our role is not simply to provide access.

We help you review the information available for each vehicle so you can make a more informed decision before authorizing a purchase.

If seeing or test-driving a vehicle before purchase is essential to your decision, this purchasing model may not be the right fit.

We prefer to make that clear from the beginning, so you know exactly what to expect before moving forward.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the car I want?

Yes. You tell me the brand, model, and budget you have in mind, and I search the auction on your behalf. You decide what fits before we place a bid.

Do I need to be a dealer to buy at auction?

No. My Car Scout already has that access, so you don't need your own dealer credential. We place the bid using our own access, on your behalf.

Can I search for vehicles myself?

No, access to the auction stays with us. You share your budget and criteria (brand, model, price range), and I bring you the best options from that day's inventory.

Do you inspect the car before I buy?

We share the auction information available for each vehicle, including photos and the vehicle's history. A CARFAX report is provided once the negotiation reaches a more concrete stage, not at first contact. We also offer an in-person inspection as an add-on service, available only at our Fort Lauderdale and Palm Beach auctions. At those locations, the vehicle can be started, but not driven or run through electronic diagnostics. Worth noting: this inspection broadens your view of the car, but it doesn't eliminate the risk inherent to an auction purchase.

Can you buy a car without my authorization?

No. No bid is placed without your authorization. You know which vehicle the bid is for, and you set the limit. You stay in control of the purchase.

Do you guarantee the car won't have problems?

No. Buying at auction involves risk, and no analysis eliminates all of it. My Car Scout helps you review the available information for a clearer decision.

Who decides how much to pay for the car?

You do. Before we participate in the auction, you set the maximum amount you're willing to offer. My Car Scout respects that limit.

If I don't win the car, can I try another?

Yes. Not winning a vehicle doesn't end your search. You can keep looking for another opportunity and decide when you want to try again. No fee for trying.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, we work with financing options, subject to the terms and approval of the financial institution. Talk to us so we can evaluate your case.

Do you handle the paperwork?

My Car Scout guides and coordinates the documentation required based on the type of purchase and where the vehicle will be registered. Each transaction may have different requirements, and we explain what applies to your case.

How do I receive the car after the purchase?

Once the process is complete, you can pick up the vehicle in person, send someone to pick it up, or hire a transport service to your desired location. You choose the best option for you.

How do I get started?

Talk to My Car Scout. We'll understand what you're looking for and show you how the process works.
